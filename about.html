About Us

Who We Are

We are a team of Miami University students enrolled in IMS 222 interested in exploring the restaurant scene in Oxford, Ohio. Our website was created to provide information about local restaurants and help students, residents, and visitors discover dining options throughout the Oxford community.

Our Purpose

Oxford has a wide variety of restaurants that offer different types of food, dining experiences, and atmospheres. Our goal is to highlight the variety of restaurants available in Oxford and make it easier for people to learn about the local dining scene.
