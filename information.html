Explore Oxford's Food Scene

Oxford, Ohio, is home to a variety of restaurants, cafés, bakeries, and bars that offer something for different tastes and occasions. From grabbing coffee and breakfast before class to enjoying dinner with friends, Oxford's food scene has a range of dining experiences to explore. This guide highlights local food spots across several categories to help students, residents, and visitors discover places to eat throughout the community.

Sit-down and Special Occasion Dining

Paesano's Pasta House

Paesano's is a cozy Italian restaurant located at 308 S Campus Ave in Oxford. The restaurant has been serving Italian food in Oxford since 2004 and offers classic Italian favorites, specialty pasta dishes, pizza, salads, and other Italian-inspired meals. Many dishes are made to order, and customers can create their own pasta dishes by choosing their pasta, sauce, and meat or seafood. Paesano's also offers gluten-free pasta, risotto, and vegetable options.

Visit Paesano's Pasta House's Website

Steinkeller

Steinkeller is an authentic German Bier Hall and restaurant located at 15 E High St in Oxford. The restaurant offers traditional German dishes such as schnitzel and spätzle, along with more than 22 different German beers. Steinkeller also offers wine, weekend brunch, and weekly specials such as steak, schnitzel, and prime rib. Its location in Oxford's historic Stone Cellar gives the restaurant a unique Bavarian atmosphere.

Visit Steinkeller's Website

The Pickle & Pig

The Pickle & Pig is a prohibition-style restaurant located inside The Elms Hotel at 77 S Main St in Oxford. Inspired by the 1930s, the restaurant combines vintage charm with handcrafted cocktails, bourbon, and elevated dishes. Its menu includes smoked pork belly, stuffed mushrooms, charcuterie boards, and other creative dishes. The Pickle & Pig also offers private events, rehearsal dinners, and other special occasions.

Visit The Pickle & Pig's Website

Sorriso Osteria & Bar

Sorriso Osteria + Bar is an Italian-American restaurant located at 26 W High St in the heart of Uptown Oxford. The restaurant serves pizza, pasta, salads, sandwiches, and shareable plates in a relaxed, low-light atmosphere. Its menu includes 16-inch pizzas and a variety of pasta dishes, with options designed for sharing. Sorriso describes itself as a place for dates, birthdays, celebrations, and longer dinners with friends or family.

Visit Sorriso Osteria's Website

Coffee, Cafés, and Breakfast

Kofenya

Kofenya is a local coffee shop located at 38 W High St in Oxford. It offers coffee, espresso drinks, teas, matcha, pastries, gourmet toast, and breakfast wraps. With its cozy and welcoming atmosphere, Kofenya is a popular place to study, meet friends, or enjoy a drink. Kofenya also has its own roasting company, Firefly Roasting, which makes it a unique part of Oxford's local coffee scene.

Visit Kofenya Coffee's Website

Bagel & Deli

Bagel & Deli is a casual bagel shop and deli located at 119 E High St in Oxford. The restaurant is known for its steamed bagel sandwiches and offers more than 90 specialty sandwich combinations, including meat, vegetarian, and breakfast options. Located less than half a block from Miami University, Bagel & Deli has a casual and lively atmosphere. It has been part of Oxford's food scene since 1975 and is a convenient option for breakfast, lunch, or a quick casual meal.

Watch this video to see why Bagel & Deli is a must-stop when visiting Oxford!

Visit Bagel & Deli's Website

Patterson's Café

Patterson's Café is an American breakfast and lunch restaurant located at 103 W Spring St in Oxford. The restaurant serves breakfast all day, including pancakes, French toast, omelets, eggs Benedict, and breakfast bowls, along with burgers, sandwiches, coffee, and other drinks. Its comfortable sit-down atmosphere makes it a place for college students, families, university staff, locals, and visitors to enjoy a meal. Patterson's also offers gluten-free and vegetarian options.

Visit Patterson Cafe's Website

TOUS les JOURS Bakery Cafe

TOUS les JOURS is a French-Asian bakery and café located at 5 W High St in Oxford. It offers freshly baked breads, cakes, pastries, donuts, macarons, sandwiches, coffee, espresso drinks, teas, matcha, and blended beverages. The modern and welcoming bakery has indoor and outdoor seating, making it a good place to enjoy coffee and pastries, meet with friends, or stop in for a quick visit. Its combination of French and Asian-inspired baking makes it a unique option in Oxford.

Visit TOUS les JOURS's Website

Its lively atmosphere makes it a casual place to spend time with friends and enjoy entertainment in Uptown Oxford.

Skipper's Pub & Top Deck

Skipper's Pub & Top Deck is a casual pub and restaurant located at 121 E High St in Oxford. The restaurant has been part of Oxford's food scene since 1984 and offers burgers, wings, gyros, salads, sandwiches, Reubens, and other pub favorites. Skipper's also serves a variety of beers and cocktails and offers weekly food and drink specials. The restaurant also provides catering for parties, graduations, and other events.

Mac & Joe's

The restaurant has been serving the Oxford community since 1946 and offers food, drinks, and entertainment. Mac & Joe's features multiple TVs for watching sports and offers lunch and dinner specials throughout the week. The casual atmosphere makes it a popular place to meet with friends and enjoy a meal or watch a game.

Phan Shin provides dine-in, carryout, and delivery options, along with lunch specials. Its menu offers a variety of Chinese and Thai dishes for different tastes.

Krishna provides dine-in, takeout, and catering services. Customers can also order online and earn points through the restaurant's rewards app.

The shop offers soft-serve ice cream, smoothies, shaved ice, and other sweet treats, along with foods such as hot dogs, pulled pork sandwiches, pretzels, and funnel cakes. Spring Street Treats also has outdoor seating, games, and a drive-thru for convenient takeout.

Visit Spring Street Treats's Website

Graeter's Ice Cream

Graeter's Ice Cream is an ice cream shop located at 29 W High St in Uptown Oxford. The shop offers a variety of Graeter's ice cream flavors and other sweet treats. Its location in the center of Oxford makes it a convenient place to stop for dessert while exploring Uptown. Customers can order in the store or through Graeter's online ordering options.

Insomnia Cookies

Insomnia Cookies is a bakery known for its warm cookies and late-night delivery. The bakery offers a variety of cookies along with brownies, cookie cakes, ice cream, and milk.
